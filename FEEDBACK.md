# Feedback

This project is young (version 0.3, functional). It will certainly be improved —
and to make sure it moves in the right direction, your perspective genuinely matters.

Whether you have **tried it**, simply **looked through it**, or work in
accessibility, BIM, or the building trade: all feedback is welcome.

## What helps me most

- Did you run it on an IFC file? What worked, what got stuck?
- Does something seem wrong, inaccurate, or incomplete?
- Is a check, a feature, or a real-world use case missing?
- Does the project feel clear, understandable, useful?
- Are you an auditor, an architect, or a concerned person? Your experience is invaluable.

Honest criticism is welcome. A project doesn't grow on compliments, but on sincere feedback.

## How to send feedback

- **On GitHub**: open an *issue* (the "Issues" tab of the repository). This is ideal
  for reporting a problem or suggesting an improvement, and it stays on record.
- **On LinkedIn**: if you prefer a more direct conversation, reach out to me here:
  https://fr.linkedin.com/in/kévin-chatellard-aa2317188

Thank you in advance for the time you take. Every piece of feedback moves the project forward.

— Kévin Chatellard · Exylia Project
